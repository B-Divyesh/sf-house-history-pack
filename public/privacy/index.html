Your house data stays at your house.

House History Pack is local-first. Property details, serial numbers, repair notes, tasks, and attached files are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB on this device. We do not receive or analyze them.

What is stored

The app stores the records and files you enter, plus your pack presentation settings. A Pack Plus license token and its most recent verification result are stored in localStorage. Removing this site’s browser data removes these records, so make encrypted backups regularly.

What leaves the device

Normal record keeping and PDF, ZIP, JSON, and encrypted backup generation happen entirely in your browser. When you buy or verify Pack Plus, your browser contacts the Sociobot billing API with the license token. Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record, processes checkout information under its own privacy terms. The app contains no analytics, advertising pixels, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts.

Your choices

You can export your data at any time. ZIP files and ordinary reports can contain private addresses, invoices, permits, serial numbers, or contractor details; store and share them carefully. Encrypted backups use AES-GCM with a key derived from your password. We cannot recover the password.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@sociobot.in. This policy may change if the product’s data practices change; the effective date above will be updated.
